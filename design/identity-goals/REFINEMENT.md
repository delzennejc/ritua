# Your life, connected

A refinement of the selected Identity board concept, using excerpts from the supplied list. Open refined-board.html. The six headings demonstrate custom Areas; Work and Personal remain the production defaults.

- Areas organize life responsibilities. Their labels can stay familiar.
- Identities are optional, editable links across Areas, goals, and practices. All proposed identity wording and associations are suggestions.
- Goals describe outcomes. Projects and tasks help produce them. Task completion does not advance outcome metrics automatically.
- Practices describe recurring behavior and can stand alone without a goal or identity.
- Small tasks remain ordinary tasks.
- The Area and Identity views reference the same objects. Linking a goal to two identities does not duplicate the goal.
- No current progress is inferred from the supplied list. Targets and original dates are preserved; unspecified cadence is left unspecified.
- All changes live in page memory. No application data is read or changed, and no external service is used.

Try changing views, editing identity wording, linking Chouette to several identities, updating an outcome, editing a practice cadence, or choosing one next step for the example weekly plan.

Regenerate from the repository root with node design/identity-goals/refine-board.mjs.
