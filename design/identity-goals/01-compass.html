Identity & Goals

A little clarity about who you’re becoming and where you’re going.

Who I want to become

My intention

Someone who makes useful things, stays curious, and is present for the people I love.

I want the way I spend my days to reflect what matters to me.

What that looks like
Learn from people, not assumptions

Make room for real conversations before building.

Share before it feels perfect

Small, useful releases are how I learn.

Give my evenings back to my family

Close work with enough energy left to be present.

This can change as you do. It’s a direction to return to, not a standard to live up to.

What I’m working toward

Bring these goals into your weekly planning. Choose the next step that supports the direction you care about.
