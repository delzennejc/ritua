# Ritua 0.1.5

Local patch release — 2026-09-19. macOS, Apple Silicon (arm64).

## Included

- Tasks now offer a contextual side menu from right-click or Shift+F10.
- The menu can add or remove calendar scheduling, assign or change a Project,
  move a task to a Horizon or Area, and open its details.
- Moving a Project-owned task to another Area asks for confirmation before
  removing its Project assignment.
- The menu works consistently on board cards and task-list rows, including
  keyboard navigation and focus restoration.

## Install

Open `Ritua-0.1.5-arm64.dmg` and drag Ritua into Applications. Quit Ritua
before replacing its bundle. Workspace data remains outside the app bundle.

This local release is unsigned and not notarized. No automatic-update channel
or public publication is configured.
